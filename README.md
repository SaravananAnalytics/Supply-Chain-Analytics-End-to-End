Supply Chain & Manufacturing Analytics PlatformBusiness ObjectiveA mid-sized to large manufacturing organization is currently facing critical operational challenges, including rising logistics costs, frequent inventory stock-outs, severe supplier delivery delays, and mounting production downtime. These bottlenecks are compounding, leading to reduced overall profitability and deteriorating customer service levels.The primary objective of this project is to architect, develop, and deploy a centralized Supply Chain & Manufacturing Analytics Platform. This platform serves to establish end-to-end operational visibility across procurement, inventory, production, and logistics. By leveraging data engineering, business intelligence, and machine learning, the solution aims to:Proactively Manage Risk: Identify and mitigate supplier delays and stock-out risks before they cause production downtime.Improve Forecasting Accuracy: Align inventory levels precisely with actual market demand to reduce holding costs and avoid shortages.Optimize Logistics & Operations: Minimize production downtime and optimize transport mode selection to reduce overhead.Drive Strategic Decisions: Provide executive stakeholders with actionable, quantifiable insights for supplier rationalization, contract renegotiation, and safety stock optimization.Project OverviewThis end-to-end analytics solution covers the entire data lifecycle using a hybrid architecture. The pipeline ingests raw operational data, performs distributed data transformations and machine learning within Databricks, structures the final reporting layer in a SQL Server data warehouse, and surfaces early-warning systems and insights through interactive Power BI dashboards.Tech StackUnified Analytics & ML: Databricks, PySpark, MLflowData Warehousing: SQL ServerData Engineering / ETL: Databricks Notebooks, PySpark, SQL Server Import Wizard, T-SQLBusiness Intelligence: Power BI, DAX, Databricks Connector for Power BIPredictive Analytics & Statistics: Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn, Statsmodels), Databricks NotebooksDataset DescriptionThe project leverages structured historical data (2019–2024) across 7 operational domains:suppliers.csv: Supplier details, regional locations, and historical ratings.purchase_orders.csv: Purchase order lifecycles, quantities, and promised vs. actual delivery timelines.inventory.csv: SKU-level stock positions, warehouse locations, and reorder thresholds.production.csv: Production output metrics, downtime hours, and categorized downtime reasons.logistics.csv: Shipment costs, transport modes, and transit delays.sales_forecast.csv: Historical demand logs and baseline forecasted demand.calendar.csv: Centralized date dimension for time-series analysis.Project Methodology & PhasesPhase 1: Database Design & Data LoadingSchema Design: Designed a robust star schema optimized for supply chain analytics, explicitly defining staging, dimension, and fact tables.Data Ingestion: Loaded raw CSV files into staging environments.Deliverables: ER Diagram, SQL DDL Scripts, and populated staging tables.Phase 2: ETL & Data Transformation (Powered by Databricks)Distributed Cleansing: Leveraged Databricks (PySpark) to process and standardize raw procurement, inventory, and logistics data at scale.
